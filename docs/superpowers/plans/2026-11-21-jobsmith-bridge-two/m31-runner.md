`linkResults` is the single writer of `work_request_artifact`. For each result it finds an artifact row of this user in the request's job (scope `opportunity`) or its company (scope `company`) with that key and version, `created_at` or `updated_at` after `request.claimedAt` unless the result's status is `unchanged`; it inserts one row (`expected` true when the key is in `KINDS[kind].expected(...)`); a result that does not check out is dropped with warning `result_unknown`.
